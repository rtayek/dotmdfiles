Perform a high-level design and architecture review.

A finding qualifies only if it materially affects one or more of:

- system responsibilities or boundaries
- data integrity
- concurrency or transaction safety
- extensibility
- unnecessary dependencies
- operational reliability
- the ability to achieve the project’s stated goals

Do not report naming conventions, formatting, comments, Java/JDK version
differences, dependency-version freshness, documentation placement, or other
maintenance issues unless they currently cause a build failure, incorrect
behavior, serious compatibility risk, or block the proposed design.

Do not repeat previously known maintenance issues.

Limit the main report to the three most consequential architectural findings.
Put any genuinely useful minor observations in a short “Deferred maintenance”
section, with no more than three items.
